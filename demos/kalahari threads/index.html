Born From the Kalahari

Kalahari Threads is a fictional brand imagined for this demonstration, built around the idea of clothing shaped by Botswana's wide horizons, warm-toned landscapes and everyday street style.

The concept draws on open skies, sun-worn colour and understated texture — translated into modern, wearable silhouettes rather than literal print or pattern.
