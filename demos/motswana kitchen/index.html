15+ Years of Tradition

Our Story

Cooked with Culture, Served with Heart

Motswana Kitchen was born out of a love for the rich culinary heritage of Botswana. What started as a small family kitchen sharing Seswaa and Bogobe with neighbours has grown into a beloved local favourite — without ever losing the heart of home cooking.

We believe great food tells a story. Every dish we serve is rooted in Setswana tradition, prepared using fresh, locally-sourced ingredients from Botswana's own farms and markets. From the way we pound our sorghum to the slow hours we spend stewing our meat, every step honours the culture and hospitality we grew up with.
